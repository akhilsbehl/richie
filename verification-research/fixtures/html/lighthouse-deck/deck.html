Section 01

Light

Range came from optics more than from fuel.

01 · Fuel

The fuel changed four times; the lens changed range far more

  1. Whale oil burned clean but grew scarce and costly.
  2. Colza and lard oil were cheaper plant and animal substitutes.
  3. Kerosene gave a brighter flame from mineral oil.
  4. Electric lamps removed the nightly refuelling task.

Sequence and dates are approximate and varied by country, from the early 1800s to the early 1900s.
